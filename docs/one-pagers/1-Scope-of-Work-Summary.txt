 WHAT WE ARE BUILDING
 A system that answers insurance certificate requests automatically. A broker
 emails asking for proof that a trucking company is insured. The system works
 out who the request is about, fills in the standard industry form from the
 agency's own records, and puts a finished document in front of a member of
 staff. That person checks it and clicks send.

 Staff are deliberately kept in the loop. The system prepares documents. It
 never issues or sends one on its own.

 WHY IT MATTERS
 Today this is done by hand: read the email, look the company up, retype
 policy numbers, coverage limits, dates and vehicle details onto a form. It
 is slow, and a typing error on an insurance certificate is a liability for
 the agency, not just an inconvenience.

 WHAT ALREADY WORKS
   - Reads the agency mailbox and ignores anything that is not a request
   - Works out which insured company the request is about
   - Emails the requester when two clients are named too alike to tell apart
   - Fills the form from the agency's records - nothing is retyped
   - Leaves vehicle ID numbers off unless the request asks for them
   - Handles large vehicle fleets that spill onto a second page
   - Puts new requests on screen by itself, within seconds, without a reload
   - Lets staff review, edit, approve and email the certificate back
   - Keeps a permanent record of who did what, which cannot be altered

 WHAT IS STILL TO DO
   - Connect to the agency's main system so policy data flows in (see below)
   - Switch the mailbox connection to a safer, revocable method
   - Add usage limits, automatic clean-up of old emails, and a records screen
   - Deploy it properly, test it with the client, train the staff

 THE ONE THING THAT BLOCKS EVERYTHING
 The system prints policy details from a database. Right now that database is
 ours, and it must be filled from the agency's real system. Until the client
 decides HOW - a live connection, a regular file transfer, or keeping it by
 hand - we cannot put dates against any later stage of the project.

 WHAT WE NEED FROM THE CLIENT
   - A licence to reproduce the industry forms (slow - start now)
   - A decision on where policy data comes from
   - A dedicated email address for certificate requests
   - Permission to connect to their email and their staff sign-in
   - Named people who will check and send certificates

 NOT INCLUDED
 Other insurance forms. Other lines of business. Quoting, billing or claims.
 Moving old certificates into the system. A phone app.

 BIGGEST RISKS
   1. The forms licence is slow and outside anyone's control
   2. The client's main system may not offer a way to connect to it
   3. If nobody is assigned to check certificates, nothing gets sent
