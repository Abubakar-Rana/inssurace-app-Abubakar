 Eighteen parts. Each does one job and hands over to the next. Listed in the
 order a request passes through them.

  #   What it does                                              Status
  -------------------------------------------------------------------------
  1   Reads the agency mailbox                                  Working (*)
  2   Ignores mail that is not a certificate request            Working
  3   Reads the company name out of the email                   Working
  4   Matches that name to a client on the agency's books       Working
  5   Asks the requester when two clients are too alike         Working
  6   Works out who to address the certificate to               Working
  7   Decides whether to print vehicle ID numbers               Working
  8   Gathers the policy, vehicle and company details           Working
  9   Lays it all onto the industry form                        Working
 10   Controls draft / issued / corrected status                Working
 11   Emails the certificate back to whoever asked              Working
 12   Watches the mailbox and pushes changes to the screen      Working
 13   The screen staff use to review and approve                Working
 14   The screen for requests the system could not identify     Working
 15   Staff sign-in and keeping agencies' data separate         Nearly (**)
 16   The permanent record of who did what                      Nearly (***)
 17   Locking away sensitive data and deleting it on time       Part-built
 18   The automated test pack that proves all of the above      Working

   (*)   Works, but connects to email in a way that is fine for our own

  (**)   Built and tested. Still needs the client to register us with their

 (***)   The record is complete and cannot be tampered with. There is no
         screen to view it on yet.

 NOT BUILT AT ALL
 A connection to the agency's main system, so that policy data flows in by
 itself. Everything above currently reads from our own database.

 THE FOUR PARTS WORTH UNDERSTANDING

 MATCHING THE COMPANY (part 4). The system will refuse to guess. If two
 clients have similar names it stops rather than picking one. That is the
 design working, not a fault. Giving us the alternative spellings the agency
 already knows about reduces how often it happens.

 ASKING THE REQUESTER (part 5). When two clients fit equally - say "Smart Way
 Solutions Inc" and "Smart Way Solutions LLC" - the system emails the person
 who asked, requesting a USDOT or MC number. Those numbers belong to exactly
 one carrier, so the answer settles it precisely. The request then completes on
 its own. THIS IS THE ONLY MESSAGE THE SYSTEM SENDS WITHOUT A PERSON, it
 contains no coverage or document, and it can be switched off.

 VEHICLE ID NUMBERS (part 7). Left off unless the request asks for them. A full
 vehicle list with VINs is an inventory of the insured's trucks, and it goes to
 a third party the insured did not choose.

 SENDING (part 11). No certificate is ever sent automatically. A person
 approves, then a person sends. The reply goes back into the original email
 conversation with the document attached.

 HOW WE KNOW IT WORKS

 236 automated checks run on demand, including 45 that drive a real web browser
 through the whole job, plus three opt-in tests that send genuine email.
 Roughly half the checks confirm the system correctly REFUSES to act when it
 should not.
